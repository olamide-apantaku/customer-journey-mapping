## What is Customer Journey Mapping

A customer journey map is a visual representation 
of every step a customer takes when interacting 
with a company — from first contact to resolution 
and beyond.

It helps support teams:
- Understand the customer experience end to end
- Identify pain points and friction in the process
- Improve touchpoints that cause frustration
- Design better support experiences proactively

## Journey Map 001 — New Customer Onboarding

**Customer Goal:** Successfully set up and use account

**Stages:**

    Awareness → Sign Up → Verification → Activation → First Use → Support

**Detailed Journey:**

| Stage | Customer Action | Customer Feeling | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Discovers product | Curious | Website, social media | Too much information | Simplify messaging |
| Sign Up | Creates account | Hopeful | Signup page | Long form | Reduce required fields |
| Verification | Submits KYC documents | Anxious | Email, app | Unclear instructions | Add step by step guide |
| Activation | Waits for approval | Impatient | Email notification | Long wait time | Send progress updates |
| First Use | Uses product for first time | Nervous | App, platform | Confusing interface | Add onboarding tutorial |
| Support | Contacts support with question | Frustrated | Email, chat, phone | Slow response | Improve first response time |

**Key Moments of Truth:**
- KYC verification experience — make it simple and fast
- First interaction with support — must be warm and helpful
- First successful transaction — celebrate this moment

**Improvement Actions:**
- Send welcome email immediately after sign up
- Provide clear KYC instructions with examples
- Send activation progress updates every 24 hours
- Assign dedicated support agent for first 30 days
- Follow up after first use to confirm satisfaction

## Journey Map 002 — Issue Resolution

**Customer Goal:** Get issue resolved quickly and painlessly

**Stages:**

    Issue Occurs → Contact Support → Agent Interaction → Resolution → Follow Up

**Detailed Journey:**

| Stage | Customer Action | Customer Feeling | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Issue Occurs | Experiences problem | Frustrated | Product, app | Unexpected issue | Proactive communication |
| Contact Support | Reaches out via channel | Anxious | Email, phone, chat | Long wait time | Reduce response time |
| Agent Interaction | Explains issue to agent | Hopeful | Agent | Repeating information | Check CRM history first |
| Resolution | Receives solution | Relieved | Agent, email | Unclear solution | Explain clearly |
| Follow Up | Receives follow up | Valued | Email, call | No follow up | Always follow up |

**Key Moments of Truth:**
- First response time — sets the tone for entire interaction
- Agent empathy — determines customer satisfaction
- Resolution quality — determines if customer returns

**Improvement Actions:**
- Acknowledge every contact within 1 hour
- Check CRM history before every interaction
- Explain resolution clearly in plain language
- Follow up within 24 hours after resolution
- Send CSAT survey immediately after closure

## Journey Map 003 — Escalation Journey

**Customer Goal:** Get complex issue resolved by the right person

**Stages:**

    Issue Identified → Agent Attempts Resolution → Escalation Decision → Team Lead Resolution → Follow Up

**Detailed Journey:**

| Stage | Customer Action | Customer Feeling | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Issue Identified | Reports complex issue | Frustrated | Agent | Not taken seriously | Show urgency immediately |
| Agent Attempts | Waits for resolution | Hopeful | Agent | Multiple attempts | Set clear timeline |
| Escalation Decision | Told issue is escalated | Worried | Agent | Being passed around | Explain escalation clearly |
| Team Lead Resolution | Interacts with Team Lead | Cautiously hopeful | Team Lead | Starting over again | Brief Team Lead fully |
| Follow Up | Receives update | Valued or Ignored | Email, call | No update | Proactive updates always |

**Key Moments of Truth:**
- Escalation communication — how you tell customer matters
- Team Lead first interaction — must not start from scratch
- Resolution speed — customer already waited too long

**Improvement Actions:**
- Never escalate without informing customer first
- Brief Team Lead fully before handover
- Update customer every 2 hours during escalation
- Resolve escalations faster than standard tickets
- Personal call from Team Lead after resolution

## Journey Map 004 — Fintech Transaction Issue

**Customer Goal:** Recover failed or incorrect transaction

**Stages:**

    Transaction Fails → Customer Contacts Support → Investigation → Resolution → Confirmation

**Detailed Journey:**

| Stage | Customer Action | Customer Feeling | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction Fails | Notices failed transaction | Panicked | App, bank | No immediate explanation | Send instant notification |
| Contacts Support | Reaches out urgently | Stressed | Phone, email, chat | Long wait time | Priority queue for transactions |
| Investigation | Waits for investigation | Anxious | Agent | No updates during investigation | Update every 30 minutes |
| Resolution | Receives refund or fix | Relieved | Agent, email | Takes too long | Resolve within 24 hours |
| Confirmation | Confirms receipt | Satisfied | Email, app | No confirmation | Always confirm in writing |

**Key Moments of Truth:**
- Speed of first response — money issues require urgency
- Investigation updates — silence causes panic
- Refund confirmation — must be clear and documented

**Improvement Actions:**
- Create priority queue for transaction issues
- Send automated acknowledgement within 15 minutes
- Update customer every 30 minutes during investigation
- Resolve transaction issues within 24 hours
- Confirm refund in writing with reference number

## Journey Map 005 — Customer Offboarding

**Customer Goal:** Close account smoothly without hassle

**Stages:**

    Decision to Leave → Contact Support → Retention Attempt → Account Closure → Post Closure

**Detailed Journey:**

| Stage | Customer Action | Customer Feeling | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decision to Leave | Decides to close account | Disappointed | Internal | Unresolved issue | Identify root cause |
| Contact Support | Requests account closure | Determined | Agent | Being talked out of it | Respect their decision |
| Retention Attempt | Listens to retention offer | Open or Closed | Agent | Aggressive retention | Gentle and genuine approach |
| Account Closure | Account is closed | Neutral | Agent, email | Complicated process | Make it simple |
| Post Closure | Receives confirmation | Closure | Email | No confirmation | Always send confirmation |

**Key Moments of Truth:**
- Retention conversation — must feel genuine not scripted
- Closure process — must be simple and fast
- Final communication — last impression matters

**Improvement Actions:**
- Always ask why customer is leaving — document feedback
- Make retention offer genuine not scripted
- Respect customer decision without pressure
- Complete closure within 24 hours of request
- Send closure confirmation with clear reference number
- Follow up 30 days later with win back offer

## Pain Points and Improvement Opportunities

| Pain Point | Journey Stage | Impact | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long response times | All journeys | High | Reduce first response to 1 hour |
| Repeating information | Issue resolution | High | Always check CRM history first |
| No proactive updates | Escalation, transaction | High | Update every 2 hours minimum |
| Unclear KYC process | Onboarding | Medium | Add step by step guide |
| Aggressive retention | Offboarding | Medium | Train agents on gentle retention |
| No follow up after resolution | All journeys | High | Mandatory follow up within 24 hours |
| Slow transaction resolution | Fintech | High | Priority queue for transactions |
| Being passed between agents | Escalation | High | Brief handover — never start from scratch |
